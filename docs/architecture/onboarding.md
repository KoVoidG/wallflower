# Wallflower Onboarding

## Purpose

The onboarding process establishes the initial business baseline that powers every feature in Wallflower.

Its goal is to collect enough high-quality information to generate accurate:

- Business Health diagnostics
- Risk Detection
- Financial simulations
- AI recommendations
- Decision Reports

Wallflower is **not** an accounting software. It only collects the information necessary to understand how the business operates and create reliable simulations.

Target onboarding completion time:

**3–8 minutes**, depending on setup type.

---

# Design Principles

## Keep it Simple

Never overwhelm users with accounting terminology.

---

## Progressive Disclosure

Only ask for information that is currently needed.

Advanced information can be completed later.

---

## Explain Every Question

Whenever financial information is requested, explain why it improves simulation accuracy.

Example

"We use this to estimate your cash runway."

---

## Flexible Setup

Different businesses have different levels of financial readiness.

Wallflower adapts its onboarding accordingly.

---

## Everything Can Be Edited Later

Nothing entered during onboarding is permanent.

Users can update their business profile at any time.

# Simulation Confidence

Every business receives a Simulation Confidence Score.

The score reflects data completeness.

### High

90–100%

Financial records are complete.

Simulation accuracy is highest.

---

### Medium

70–89%

Some values are estimated.

Results remain reliable.

---

### Low

Below 70%

Critical information is missing.

Wallflower explains which data should be added to improve future simulations.

# AI Rules

AI never invents financial values.

AI only receives deterministic outputs generated by Wallflower.

AI responsibilities

Explain results

Summarize findings

Identify risks

Suggest actions

Highlight uncertainty

Missing information always lowers simulation confidence.

---

# UX Guidelines

Each onboarding screen should focus on one topic.

Display a progress indicator throughout the flow.

Avoid walls of text.

Prefer cards, radio buttons, dropdowns, and segmented controls over long forms.

Explain financial terms using simple language suitable for retail SME owners.

Users should finish onboarding feeling that Wallflower understands their business—not that they completed a tax form.
