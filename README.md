# Wallflower

Wallflower is an AI-powered decision-intelligence platform designed to help Thai SMEs understand business health, identify risks, and simulate the possible impact of important business decisions.

## Current Status

Wallflower is currently under active development.

## Core Capabilities

- Business health assessment
- Financial and operational risk detection
- Business decision simulation
- Scenario comparison
- AI-assisted explanations
- Decision reports and action plans

## Technology

- Next.js
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Prisma
- Neon PostgreSQL
- Better Auth
- Google Gemini
