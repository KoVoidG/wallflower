# Wallflower

> AI-powered decision intelligence platform for Thai SMEs.

Wallflower helps small and medium-sized businesses understand their financial health, identify operational risks, simulate business decisions, and receive AI-assisted recommendations before taking action.

---

## Vision

Wallflower enables business owners to make informed decisions through simulation rather than intuition.

Instead of asking:

> "What should I do?"

Wallflower answers:

> "If you do this, here are the likely financial and operational outcomes."

---

## MVP Features

- Business onboarding
- Financial health dashboard
- Business risk analysis
- Scenario simulator
- AI-powered explanations
- Business reports
- Funding readiness assessment
- Dark mode
- Responsive UI

### Initial Simulation Scenarios

- Hire Employees
- Change Product Pricing
- Marketing Investment
- Purchase Equipment
- Customer Payment Delay
